# Finance Creator Editing Workflow — 1.7.0

The current default is ChatCut finance editing with supplied original A-roll and an approved script. Preserve the original voice and wording. Deliver a real editable multitrack ChatCut project; a flattened video or JSON plan is not a substitute. Follow the [current SOP](../finance-creator-editing-workflow/references/chatcut-zhinan-finance.md) and [Chinese prompt report](PROMPT.zh-CN.md).

Use 1080x1920 at 30 fps, five narrative stages and an 85–92 second target with a complete closing. Use a centered presenter or presenter window over visible B-roll, restrained dark grading, hard cuts and fades, whole-sentence Chinese/English captions and speech-timed yellow keyword wipes. Update meaningful information every 8–12 seconds in the 40–75 second explanation section. Do not impose historical 3-second cuts, opening variety SFX, AI inserts or a 0.4-second cover hold.

Preserve original speech, use licensed restrained instrumental music, and keep impact cues at least 8 seconds apart. Initial track gains of -6 dB speech and -18 dB music are not LUFS targets; inspect the actual mix. Respect per-project exceptions. Run technical and editorial reviews with actual listening evidence. One active editor, short state card and incremental changes; no promise of fixed token savings. Reload the installed release when resuming, prepare migration, and check evidence before export. Old approved edits are not automatically rerendered or published. Private media, voice models, logs and credentials are excluded.
